## Core Mechanic: A Spirit-Weapon Path

A Shinigami plan connects sword practice with a developing spirit weapon. Record your abilities and organize future goals into four stages, checking requirements as you play:

- **Starting character** — Learn movement and sword rhythm before changing your build.
- **Weapon development** — Record your current abilities and the attributes you can observe.
- **First release** — Prepare for an ability milestone using the game's current requirements.
- **Advanced release** — Revisit later challenges as they become available. Date your checklist and confirm each requirement before committing resources.

## Prepare an Ability Unlock

Prepare requirements, practice, and an encounter plan. Use this checklist as a framework and confirm unlock conditions in game.

1. **Check current requirements** in the relevant dialogue and record them.
2. Practice movement, blocking, and recovery in a familiar encounter.
3. Inspect equipment and improve one weak point.
4. Record instructions and clues separately from guesses.
5. Prepare your encounter practice in three stages:
   - **Observe** — Watch the opening pattern before choosing your response.
   - **Adapt** — Practice a consistent response across several attempts.
   - **Review** — Adjust one part of the plan after each attempt.

Consult this checklist between encounters, updating requirements as you confirm them through play.

## Close-Range Practice ⭐ Planning Notes

Use a training log to understand movement and recovery. Separate combat practice from ability unlocks or skill-point investments. Choose one session goal and compare repeated attempts to check whether your response stays consistent.

- **Practice goal:** Repeat one action in a familiar encounter.
- **Observation log:** Record distance, timing, and the response you saw.
- **Next adjustment:** Change one part of your approach and compare results before changing another. A short log makes patterns easier to recognize.

*These original practice prompts do not assert a live training quest, badge, or completion statistic.*
