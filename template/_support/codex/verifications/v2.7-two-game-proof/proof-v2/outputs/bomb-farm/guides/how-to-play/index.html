How to Play Bomb Farm: The Mine and Three-Team Loop

Observe the mine, inspect upgrade opportunities and switch between three teams using the recorded official concept, without invented recovery timers.

Start by observing the bomber squad’s mine progress, then inspect an available upgrade and the teams you can select. The recorded official description supports switching between three teams to keep the mine running; it does not prescribe when to switch or a mandatory order.

Read the three-team diagram

The three cards above represent three team choices, not three stages of a recovery cycle. TEAM 1, TEAM 2 and TEAM 3 are editorial labels. The shared mine task connects them; the picture does not show simultaneous team activity, cooldowns or energy values.

Use it to understand the switching concept, then inspect the actual state in the current game before acting. It is an explanatory diagram, not a screenshot or a simulator.

Follow one mine session

  1. Observe the current push. Check whether the mine and squad are progressing through auto battles and stages.
  2. Inspect the rewards. Look for an upgrade opportunity in the systems available to you; the recorded store description names equipment, heroes and account-level progression.
  3. Check your team choices. Consider switching between the three teams using the current interface. Do not infer that a numbered team must act next.
  4. Observe the result. Recheck whether progress continues and whether the next decision is clear. Change one decision at a time so you can tell what happened.

This sequence is editorial guidance for observation. It is not a sourced optimal build order or a guarantee that switching fixes a stalled run.

Between pushes: inspect rather than guess

The recorded store description names gear, Forge upgrades and a skill tree. Their presence gives you places to inspect an upgrade opportunity; it does not establish which upgrade is best for your current squad.

If you cannot tell what an action changes, stop before spending resources and inspect the current details. Do not substitute a guessed cost, recovery rule or damage formula.

Know when this guide has answered the question

You have the bounded answer when you can locate the mine progress, identify a decision to inspect and explain what the three-team switching concept means. You do not need to know a timer or adopt an optimal build to finish this guide.

If progress remains unclear after a change, return to observation and check the game details. This page cannot diagnose every stalled run.

Your next check

Read the official Steam description if you need to compare the current game with the source. Return to the mine overview to revisit this bounded route. This proof does not offer a separate build, Market or timer tool.

The source record used here was accessed on 4 September 2026. Writing this guide on 8 October does not mean the source or current mechanics were reverified on that date.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Does three-team switching establish an exact recovery timer?

No. The recorded official description supports switching between three teams to keep the mine running. It does not establish recovery rates, readiness states or an optimal rotation order.

When should I stop and check the current game?

Stop when the available upgrade, team state or action is unclear. Inspect the current interface and official information rather than assuming a timing or best-build rule.
