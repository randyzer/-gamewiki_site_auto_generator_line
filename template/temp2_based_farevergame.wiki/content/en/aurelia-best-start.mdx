## Learn the basics

Take your time with the opening practice area. Try movement, a simple attack and a dodge before following the next trail.

## Your first town route

Walk the town once, note the supply stops and mark a route back to safety. Read the available task descriptions, then choose one nearby outing.

## Spend with a clear purpose

Keep the equipment you understand and save unfamiliar purchases for later. Compare what you already carry before making room for another item.
